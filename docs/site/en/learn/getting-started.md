# Getting started

## Open PyQuest

Open your PyQuest instance in a recent browser (Firefox, Chrome, Edge). The Python engine loads on first start
(a few seconds); after that the app remembers everything and works **offline**.

You land on your **first unfinished level**. To open a specific level, use the **Levels** button (see below) or a
direct link like `https://…/#/levels/05-decide`.

## A level, step by step

A level is a series of steps shown one after the other:

1. **Explanation**: a short text. When a **Listen** button is present, a voice reads the text (never
   automatically: you click).
2. **Code exercise**: an instruction and a pre-filled code cell. You edit, run, and see the result and the
   validation.
3. **Final mission**: a small program that brings together what you just learned. Completing it finishes the level.

The **Continue** button moves to the next step. The **Previous level** and **Next level** buttons at the top
change level.

!!! tip "Don't panic"
    Mistakes are normal: the error is explained, and you can ask for **hints** (from vague to precise) or, as a
    last resort, show the **solution**.

## The "Levels" button

It opens a panel with:

- the **path map**: each level is a dot, with its state written out in words ("Done", "In progress", "To do");
- the **badge shelf**: badges you earned (with the date) and those still to unlock.

Choosing a level on the map opens it directly: nothing is locked.

## The guide character

A little character encourages you after a success, offers a hint when you are stuck, and welcomes you back after a
break. It never judges. You can hide it in the [settings](accessibility.md).
